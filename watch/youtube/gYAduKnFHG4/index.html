N.Flying, “a must-listen band,” is coming back with its new album on October 8th. “Still,” N.Flying’s 9th mini album, tells the story of “emotions that still remain even after a long time.” Time changes many things, but there are things that do not disappear easily. The emotions that are still alive, the emotions of still liking someone or something, and the bonds that still go on. N.Flying conveys the emotions that do not change even after a long time and still remain inside you like embers. “Still,” the title track, is a song of rock genre with weighty rhythm session and the lyrical sentiment of the guitar riff. Its lyrics tell the story of “me” still being the same as before and still keeping the passionate emotions, even though “you” do not feel the same as “I” do. The music video portrays the story of the members of N.Flying reuniting after living in different timelines and starting one flame together. The members, who have been keeping embers that were seemingly dying down, face each other’s memories and emotions, and realize that the music and faith they had for each was what kept them burning until the end. The five members, who have been taking their own paths, gather again, their small embers meet and turn into one big flame.
