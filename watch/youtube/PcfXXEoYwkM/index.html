AMPERS&ONE (KAMDEN, BRIAN, JIHO, SIYUN, KYRELL, MACKIAH, and SEUNGMO), who have been sharing their distinctive music and energy on stages around the world, have released their first full-length album three years after their debut. Their first full-length album, ‘PATHFINDER,’ captures AMPERS&ONE’s journey of forging new paths and growing along the way as they aim high while remaining humble. Having established their identity and direction through their fourth mini album, ‘DEFINITION,’ the group now takes another step forward in search of a path uniquely their own. Rather than following a predetermined route, AMPERS&ONE seeks answers for themselves, gaining a wide range of experiences from the ground up and embracing the journey itself as part of their growth.
